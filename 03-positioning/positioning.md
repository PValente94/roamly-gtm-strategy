# Positioning Framework & Statement · Roamly Groups

## 1. Positioning framework

- **For (target audience):** Friend groups aged between 18 and 35 planning a holiday together who want to easily book trusted local experiences without the complexity of managing separate accounts, coordinating payments, or piecing together itineraries.
- **Who want / need:** Who want to seamlessly and safely book a group experience without the burden of one person having to pay upfront and then settle payments per group member.
- **We offer:** We offer a seamless travel group booking experience that allows to book your activity with shared itineraries and split payments without needing to leave the booking platform.
- **Because of (value proposition):** Because it allows cash flow constrained people to book experiences without needing to move funds around first.
- **Despite (barriers):** The fact that you need all members of the group to perform the payment to really lock your booking.
- **Better than (differentiation):** Better than one person payment booking platforms, we allow flexible group bookings that suit your finances.

## 2. Positioning statement

### First draft
Roamly Groups lets friend aged 18-35 to book trusted local experiences together, with shared itineraries and split payments - so no one pays upfront for the whole group.

### Pressure test
- **Test I ran:** A · Audience recognition
- **What it revealed:** Mostly recognized itself  in the positioning statement but: "trusted local experiences" felt a little broad and "no one pays upfront for the whole group" sounds a bit formal for planning a trip with friends.

### Final statement
Roamly Groups lets friend aged 18-35 to book local cultural experiences together wherever they travel, with shared itineraries and split payments - so everyone can pay their own share instead of one person paying for the whole party.
