# Positioning Framework & Statement · Roamly Groups

## 1. Positioning framework

- **For (target audience):** Friend groups aged between 21 and 35 planning a holiday together who want to easily book trusted local experiences without the complexity of managing separate accounts, coordinating payments, or piecing together itineraries.
- **Who want / need:** Who want to seamlessly and safely book a group experience without the burden of one person having to pay upfront and then settle payments per group member.
- **We offer:** Roamly Groups is a collaborative booking experience that enables groups to discover, organize, and book authentic local experiences together. Shared itineraries, group coordination, and split payments are built directly into the booking flow, eliminating the need to manage plans, payments, and communication across multiple tools and platforms.
- **Because of (value proposition):** Roamly Groups makes group travel easier by removing the financial and logistical burden typically placed on a single organizer. Instead of one person paying upfront and collecting money from everyone afterwards, participants can contribute directly, reducing friction, improving transparency, and making booking large group experiences more accessible for travelers with limited cash flow.
- **Despite (barriers):** The biggest challenge is that a booking can only be fully confirmed once all required participants complete their payment. This introduces dependency on the responsiveness of the group, creating a risk that bookings remain incomplete or availability changes before everyone commits.
- **Better than (differentiation):** Better than one person payment booking platforms, we allow flexible group bookings that suit your finances.

## 2. Positioning statement

### First draft
Roamly Groups helps groups of friends discover, coordinate, and book authentic local experiences together, with shared itineraries and split payments built directly into the booking experience. By removing the need for one person to pay upfront and manage the group's finances, it makes group travel planning simpler, more transparent, and more accessible.

### Pressure test
- **Test I ran:** A · Audience recognition
- **What it revealed:** What doesn't resonate

❌ "Discover, coordinate, and book authentic local experiences together"

This sounds like marketing language.

I don't wake up thinking:

"I need a platform to coordinate authentic local experiences."

I think:

"I need a way to get this booking done without chasing seven people for money."

The pain is much more concrete.

❌ The biggest problem appears too late

The real hook is buried in the second sentence:

"By removing the need for one person to pay upfront..."

Honestly, that's the first thing I'd lead with.

That's the pain I immediately recognize.

❌ "More accessible" feels generic

Accessible to whom?

Why?

As a customer, I don't naturally connect "accessible" to "split payments."

"Fairer," "easier," or "no one gets stuck fronting the bill" are much more meaningful.

### Final statement
Tired of being the person who has to pay for everyone and chase friends for money? Roamly Groups lets groups book authentic local experiences together, with split payments, shared itineraries, and group coordination built directly into the booking experience.
