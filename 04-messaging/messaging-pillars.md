# Messaging Pillars · Roamly Groups

## 1. From/To market shift

- **From, life before:** Planning a group experience means one person becomes the organizer, collecting money, coordinating decisions, chasing confirmations, and keeping everyone informed across multiple apps and conversations.
- **To, life after:** Booking a group experience becomes a shared process, where everyone can see the plans, contribute their payment, and stay aligned from booking through the experience.

## 2. Audiences

- **Number of audiences that need their own message:** 2
- **Why you picked this number:** Group travel involves two distinct users with different motivations:

Organizers bear the burden of coordinating, booking, and paying on behalf of others.
Participants want visibility into plans and an easy way to contribute without relying entirely on the organizer.

Each audience experiences a different pain point and therefore requires a different message.

## 3. Messaging pillars

### Audience 1: The person who usually organizes and books experiences on behalf of the group.
- **Value prop:** Spend less time booking for your travel group and more time enjoying the experience with them.
- **Capabilities:** - Split payments across participants
- Shared itineraries
- Centralized booking management
- Group visibility into booking status
- **Evidence:** - 92% of organizers say booking with the group is more fun 
- reduces organizers time spent coordinating bookings and payments in 58%
- 107 verified group organizers came back within 6 months to book a new group experience

### Audience 2: the travel joiners, not organizers
- **Value prop:** Always know what's planned and pay your share with confidence. Roamly Groups gives every participant visibility into the booking, itinerary, and payment status, making it easier to contribute and stay informed without relying on the organizer for updates.
- **Capabilities:** - Split payments across participants
- Shared itineraries
- Centralized booking management
- Group visibility into booking status
- **Evidence:** - 96% of joiners feel more aligned with travel plans.
- 89% regularly check Roamly Groups for trip updates.
- 98% say they always know what they need to pay and when.
