# Competitive Intelligence & Strategic Bet · Roamly Groups

# 1. Primary Competitor

- **Competitor:** Airbnb Experiences
- **Core offering:** Offer an activities marketplace hosted by local Airbnb hosts, including local tours, cooking classes, workshops, outdoor activities, etc.
- **How they position themselves:** A way to connect with a destination through local hosts.
- **Primary audience:** Travelers looking for activities hosted by local experts, often alongside an Airbnb stay.
- **Known pricing or packaging:** Guests pay per experience, at a price set by the host, and Airbnb deducts around 20% from the host's price. The payment needs to be done in one transaction, so one person pays for the entire group.
- **Why they're the biggest threat:** They can bundle accommodation, which feels more personal than a hotel, with local activities and centralize the experience through a single provider, minimizing coordination effort and optimizing logistics.

# 2. Battlecard

# Airbnb Experiences Competitive Battlecard

## Know the Landscape

### Competitor Overview

Airbnb Experiences is a marketplace for local activities hosted by local experts. It competes with Roamly for travelers looking to discover and book experiences.

### Key Offerings

- Tours
- Food experiences
- Classes
- Outdoor activities

Airbnb supports:

- Private group bookings
- Guest invitations
- Group messaging
- Shared trip itineraries

### Pricing

Hosts set the price per guest and can offer group discounts or set a minimum price for private groups.

Airbnb typically deducts a **20% service fee** from the host's payout.

For one experience reservation, **one person pays the full amount**; guests cannot split payment across multiple payment methods.

---

## Understand the Battlefield

### Key Strengths

- Familiar travel brand with an established marketplace of local experiences.
- Group booking, invitations, messaging, and shared itineraries.
- Connects experiences with accommodation and broader trip planning.

### Key Weaknesses

- One person pays for the full experience reservation, leaving the group to settle up separately.
- Group planning tools help coordinate details like itineraries but don't eliminate the organizer's payment burden.

---

## Win the Deal

### Why You Win

Roamly Groups lets friends coordinate bookings and split payments in one place, addressing the work Airbnb leaves to the group organizer.

### Why They Win

Airbnb may win when travelers:

- Already use Airbnb for accommodation.
- Prefer its familiar brand.
- Are comfortable having one person pay and collect contributions afterward.

### Why You

Emphasize the combination of:

- Trusted local experiences
- Collaborative group booking
- Split payments

Roamly's advantage is strongest when it delivers:

- Reliable availability
- A seamless shared booking experience

---

## Handle the Hard Moments

### Quick Dismiss

> "Airbnb supports group bookings and messaging. Roamly Groups also helps the group split the payment, so one person doesn't have to pay for everyone upfront."

### Landmine Questions

- "Who pays upfront when your group books an experience?"
- "How do you collect everyone's share and keep the booking details in sync?"
- "What happens when someone changes plans?"

### Objection Handling

**"We already use Airbnb."**

> "That makes sense, especially if you're booking accommodation there. Roamly is useful when coordinating and paying for an experience as a group creates extra work."

# 3. What I Want to Revisit in My Module 1 Strategy

I would like to revisit the positioning to reinforce that Roamly Groups is not an accommodation provider, nor is it marketed as one.

While some locally curated experiences may include accommodation as part of a package, the primary focus is on the local experiences themselves, whatever form those experiences take.

# 4. Strategic Bet

## Data

### Who Roamly Groups Is For

I am keeping the target audience:

> Friend groups aged 21–35 planning a holiday together who want to easily book trusted local experiences without the complexity of managing separate accounts, coordinating payments, or piecing together itineraries.

Why this segment:

- Younger people often have less disposable income and cash flow available.
- Paying for an entire group upfront can therefore be a significant barrier.
- They are digital natives and are more likely to adopt a split-payment booking experience naturally.

### The One Thing Worth Choosing It For

> The ability for group members to pay separately while allowing the group to complete a single booking journey with minimal effort.

### The Biggest Threat to That Position

Airbnb Experiences already has:

- A stronger brand
- A larger user base
- Group coordination tools
- Shared itineraries
- Availability management
- Centralized trip planning

If Airbnb introduces native split payments, Roamly risks losing its core differentiation.

---

## Insight

Make the experience a one-stop experience by ensuring the package includes both accommodation and local cultural experiences.

---

## Belief

I now believe that Roamly Groups wins when groups of young people want to book together but don't want the burden of coordinating payment among themselves.

Young travelers often have fewer financial resources but are highly comfortable with digital experiences, making split-payment booking a natural fit for their behavior and expectations.

---

## Bet

### I Am Betting That Roamly Groups Wins By

Becoming the easiest way for groups of friends to discover, coordinate, and pay for authentic local experiences.

By eliminating the need for a single organizer to manage bookings and front the entire cost, Roamly can unlock a major source of unmet demand while staying true to its community-driven roots and focus on locally curated experiences.

### To Make This Bet, I Am Deprioritizing

My trade-off is clear: I will prioritize friend groups seeking authentic, locally curated experiences, even if that means deprioritizing larger opportunities such as corporate offsites, team-building events, bachelor and bachelorette parties, or other highly commercialized group travel categories. Even if future data comes to suggest these segments offer a larger addressable market, pursuing them would pull Roamly away from its core differentiator: helping travelers discover unique local experiences through trusted recommendations and community-driven discovery. 

I am choosing differentiation and brand alignment over market breadth.
